How to Play Tennis Legacy

A practical introduction to the first steps in Tennis Legacy: Rise to Glory, from creating your player to building a competitive tennis career.

1. Create a player

Register for a Tennis Legacy account and create your tennis player. Your player is the center of your career, so get familiar with the overview and player profile.

2. Review player skills

Study your player’s strengths and development areas. Use the Training page to plan improvement, keeping fatigue and preparation in mind as the calendar advances.

3. Plan the season

Use the calendar and schedule tools to review available competitions. Select tournaments that suit your current ambitions and your player’s condition.

4. Prepare match tactics

Before a match, consider the opponent and the tournament situation. Review the available preparation and tactical options rather than relying on one strategy for every event.

5. Manage the support team

Staff, equipment and finances contribute to the wider management experience. Check those pages regularly as your player develops.

6. Learn from results and rankings

Follow tournament results, statistics and rankings to evaluate progress. Adjust future preparation and tournament plans as your career continues.
