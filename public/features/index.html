Tennis Legacy: Rise to Glory

Tennis Legacy Features

Tennis Legacy combines tennis player development, tournament management and career decisions in one browser-based sports management game.

Player skills and training

Develop your player through training choices and long-term skill improvement. Balance preparation, conditioning and recovery as the season moves forward.

ATP and WTA competition

Take part in a tennis career world with separate men’s and women’s competitive environments, including tournaments and rankings.

Match tactics

Prepare for opponents and plan match strategy before competing. Your decisions influence how you approach each fixture.

Tournament scheduling

Choose competitions and organize a season around opportunities, player condition and career objectives.

Equipment and support

Manage tennis equipment and supporting staff as part of the player’s development and match preparation.

Finance and career choices

Follow your budget, make career decisions and consider the longer-term consequences of how you manage your player.

Rankings and progress

Track your progress through career statistics, tournament results and the broader tennis ranking environment.

Multiplayer management

Develop your player in a persistent online tennis world alongside other managers.
