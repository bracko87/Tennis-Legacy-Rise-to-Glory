Tennis Legacy: Rise to Glory

About Tennis Legacy

Tennis Legacy is a persistent online tennis management game about building a professional player’s career through decisions on and off the court.

Build a professional tennis career

Start with your own player and make the decisions that shape their growth. Training, match preparation, equipment, finances and tournament choices all matter in the game’s long-term career experience.

Rather than controlling every stroke like an arcade tennis game, you manage the career, development and strategy behind the player.

ATP and WTA career worlds

The game includes men’s ATP and women’s WTA career environments, each focused on tournament progression, competition and tennis rankings. Players can plan their schedules and work toward stronger results over time.

A shared online tennis world

Tennis Legacy brings career management into a persistent multiplayer setting where managers develop players and compete alongside others as the game world progresses.

Tennis Legacy: Rise to Glory is developed and published by Next Quest Studio.
