## English: Short description

Build your tennis career in an online multiplayer management simulation. Train your player, enter tournaments, climb the world rankings, manage sponsors, staff and finances, and shape a life beyond the court.

## English: About This Game

**Your tennis career is more than one match.**

In Tennis Legacy: Rise to Glory, you create a professional tennis player and build a career in a shared online world. Rather than controlling individual shots on the court, you make the decisions that influence your player's development and competitive journey.

**Build your player**
Develop your skills through training, choose your playing approach and plan the next steps in your career.

**Compete for a place in the rankings**
Choose tournaments, follow draws and results, earn ranking points and work your way through the professional calendar. Compete in a shared world with other human players and AI opponents.

**Manage the full career**
Hire staff, handle equipment and sponsorship decisions, keep track of finances, and make choices about your player's private life.

**One online tennis world**
The browser and planned Steam editions connect to the same multiplayer game world. Online play requires an internet connection. Matches are simulated: this is a career-management game, not an action tennis game.

**Free to play**
The main multiplayer game is free. Optional Premium features may be available through supported platform purchases; final Steam purchase support is still in preparation.

Developed by Next Quest Studio.
