Tennis Legacy: Rise to Glory — Online Tennis Management Game

Tennis Legacy is a free, browser-based tennis career management game. Create your own tennis player and shape a professional career through training, match tactics, tournament selection and long-term decisions. The game focuses on managing a player, not controlling individual shots like an arcade tennis game.

Build and develop your tennis player

Choose how your player trains, manage conditioning and recovery, and prepare for upcoming matches. Your player develops over time while you balance equipment, support staff and finances with the demands of the season.

Plan tournaments and climb the rankings

Explore the ATP and WTA career worlds, choose tournaments to enter and plan match tactics for different opponents. Follow results and rankings as the shared online tennis world progresses. Your decisions about scheduling and preparation affect the direction of your tennis career.

Play online with other tennis managers

Tennis Legacy is a persistent multiplayer tennis management simulation playable in a web browser. Registration is open: create an account and tennis player to prepare your career. Existing players can sign in to continue. Learn more about the game before you join.
